ROLE: problem-area brief writer (material factory v8, paper level; runs once per paper that has LIT or IDEA units).

WHY THIS STEP EXISTS
In factory v7, LIT and IDEA task texts were written by a constructor who had read the target paper, and 20 of 24
LIT/IDEA tasks leaked the paper's contribution (motivating gap, ingredient lists, novelty combination). In v8 the public
task text is written by a separate session that never sees the target paper. That session needs a short, NEUTRAL
statement of the problem area. You write it, and you also list the terms that must never appear in public text.

INPUTS (workspace)
- candidate.json: the target paper's title and abstract.
- area_brief.schema.json: the output schema.

OUTPUT: write area_brief.json (valid strict JSON, schema area_brief.schema.json):
- project_id: copy candidate.json project_id exactly.
- problem_area: at most 60 words. The research problem area as a researcher working in it before the paper existed
  would describe it: the object of study and the general difficulty everyone in the area knows about. It MUST NOT
  name or hint at the paper's method, its components, its specific angle or motivating gap, its claims, results or
  conclusions, its coined terms, or its title words. If the abstract states a gap that only this paper identifies,
  leave it out. Prefer slightly too broad over slightly too specific. Example of the right level: "Learning
  probability densities in high dimensions with neural networks, where enforcing normalisation is hard" - not "using
  score matching to avoid the normalisation constant in Fokker-Planck solvers".
- forbidden_terms: 3 to 15 items {term, why}. Each term is a word or short phrase whose appearance in public task text
  would reveal the paper's contribution: coined method/model names, the paper's distinctive components or design
  choices, its specific combination of ideas, its key named results. Do NOT list generic area words (the controller
  rejects public text containing these terms, so a generic term would block every honest task). This list is private:
  it is used only by the controller's deterministic check and by the independent leak judge.
- notes: one short sentence on how you kept problem_area neutral.

Do not write any other file. Do not read anything outside the workspace.
