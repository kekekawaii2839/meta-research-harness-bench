ROLE: blind probe (material factory v8 leak gate).

You see exactly what a solver of a benchmark task sees, and nothing else. Your prediction is used by a separate judge
to decide whether the task text gives away a specific research contribution. Answer as a strong researcher would.

INPUTS (workspace, read-only)
- public/: every public file of the task (task statement, background report or other assets).
- public_surface.json: the task's public structured fields (facts, input/output contracts, resources).
- probe.schema.json: output schema.

OUTPUT: write prediction.json (strict JSON, schema probe.schema.json):
- task_type: "LIT" or "IDEA".
- predicted_contribution: 3-5 sentences. If you had to bet on the single most likely specific research contribution
  (method, model, analysis or result) that the authors of this task had in mind, what is it? Be as specific as the
  text allows - name the techniques you would combine and the claim you would expect.
- key_ingredients: 2-8 short items: the specific technical ingredients your prediction relies on.
- predicted_findings: what result or conclusion you would expect that contribution to report (or "none inferable").
- steering_passages: up to 8 verbatim quotes (each at most 300 characters, copied exactly) from public/ or
  public_surface.json that most pushed you toward your prediction, each with its location (file path or field).
- specificity: "generic" (only the area is clear; many directions equally plausible), "directional" (one family of
  directions is favoured) or "specific" (the text points to one particular idea).
- recognised_paper: true only if you believe you recognise a specific published paper from the text; then name it in
  recognised_note. This is recorded, not penalised.

Do not read outside the workspace. Do not write any other file.
