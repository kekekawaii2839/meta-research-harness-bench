ROLE: background screener (material factory v8.1, paper level; once per paper that has LIT or IDEA units).

WHY THIS STEP EXISTS
A separate session that has NOT seen the target paper will write the public LIT/IDEA task text from a set of background
papers. In the v8 pilot, two IDEA tasks still leaked the target contribution because the background set contained
side-field papers that were chosen only because they supply the target method's ingredients (for example image-model
score matching for a PDE solver, or active-learning loss prediction for a sampler). The writer summarised them
faithfully, and the summary pre-assembled the answer. You decide which background papers the writer may see.

INPUTS (workspace, read-only)
- area_brief.json: problem_area (the neutral research problem area) and forbidden_terms (the target paper's distinctive
  ingredients; private).
- background/: for each background work bgNNN its arXiv abstract page (bgNNN_abs_page.html) and, when available, its
  full text. background_list.json lists each work (group id, title, arXiv id/version, version date).
- bg_screen.schema.json: output schema.

DECISION per background work (group id bgNNN):
- keep: a researcher working on problem_area before the cutoff would routinely read and cite it - core methods,
  standard baselines, benchmarks, surveys, the main competing families of the area itself.
- exclude: it belongs to another field or a narrow side topic AND its main relevance here is that it supplies one of the
  forbidden_terms (an ingredient of the target method) - i.e. a neutral survey of problem_area would not include it.
- When unsure, prefer keep unless the work is clearly outside the area and maps onto a forbidden term. Do not exclude a
  work only because it is strong or recent; exclude only side-field ingredient supplies.

OUTPUT: write bg_screen.json (strict JSON, schema bg_screen.schema.json): project_id (copy from area_brief.json),
decisions (one per group id in background_list.json: group, keep true/false, reason - one sentence; for exclude, name
the forbidden term it supplies), notes. Read the abstract pages; open full texts only when the abstract is unclear.
Do not write any other file. Do not read outside the workspace.
