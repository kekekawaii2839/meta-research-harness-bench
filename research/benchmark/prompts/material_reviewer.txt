IMPORTANT EXP AUTONOMY: This benchmark deliberately evaluates experimental design
as part of EXP. Distinguish three different things:
(1) Required fixed scientific specification: research object, exact proposed
method semantics, initialization where essential, assumptions/regime, and scoped
hypotheses. Missing information here is a real material defect.
(2) Required platform contract: package/task CPU, memory, disk, process/time,
tool/data/network limits, common across compared solvers of the same task.
Missing or unverified limits belong to environment/runtime integration, not a
requirement to supply the experimental answer.
(3) Legitimate solver decisions WITHIN that contract: datasets/settings, seed
schedule, repetitions, step-size/rate grids, iteration allocation, diagnostics,
fit windows, uncertainty analyses and tolerances. These are intentionally chosen
and justified by the solver. Their freedom alone is NOT an ambiguity, scientific
defect, or reason to reject materials. Do not demand fixed repetitions, exact
iteration counts, universal grids or identical tolerances just for comparability.
A fixed method may require positive/constant steps or rank assumptions; specify
those semantics without preselecting all experimental values. Flag only an
actual contradiction, omitted defining condition or inability to construct a
valid informative CPU experiment under the package limits, and give evidence.
